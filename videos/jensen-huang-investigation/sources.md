## Open questions

- The public materials establish one leg of a documented server movement to Malaysia. The final destination, ownership, and use of particular machines beyond that leg require separate evidence. Keep the illustrated route’s uncertain section unlabelled.
- U.S. intelligence suspicions were reported by journalists. Public evidence reviewed for this episode leaves espionage, the transfer of confidential information, and any causal link to specific chip approvals unresolved. China’s denial and the board’s stated assessment stay in the story.
- The reported $7.5 billion shipment figure is a separate reporting estimate. Evidence for a transfer of Nvidia stock to Alice Huang was not found. Neither claim is used in the spoken script. The board inquiry, federal and state investigations, and congressional interviews should be rechecked before publication.
- The inspection drawings, filenames and shipment paperwork are editorial illustrations. Every official-looking facsimile should carry an illustration label. The script has no invented dialogue or reenacted private interactions.
