**The Investigation That Reached Nvidia’s CEO**

Federal investigators were asking about advanced chips at a Singapore cloud company in 2025. More than a year later, allegations connected to the executive they interviewed put Nvidia’s leadership under scrutiny. This Dirty Dollar documentary follows the records from the hardware inspection to the Nvidia board and explains why an index-fund investor might be exposed to the outcome.
