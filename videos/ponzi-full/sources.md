## Estimates and uncertainties

- Ponzi's $2.50 is his own account. The voyage is depicted as an arrival card, with no reconstructed personal details.
- Contemporary and later reports give different investment totals. The narration attributes the roughly $15 million intake to the Smithsonian.
- Accounts distinguish the 90-day term of some notes from the 45-day pitch and early payments. The video uses the 45-day promise documented by the Smithsonian and New England Historical Society.
- The exact coupon inventory, recovery rate, total losses, and private conversations are uncertain. The cut uses the documented mismatch and omits the numerical coupon and loss estimates.
- The $100-to-$150 illustration is the advertised 50% return applied to a round example. It does not represent a verified customer's payout.
