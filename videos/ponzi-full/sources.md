## Not checked / uncertain (please note)
1. **"50% in 45 days"** - most cited; some sources say 90 days. Smithsonian could not be opened from this session (blocked) - checked via search results.
2. **"$2.50 on arrival"** is Ponzi's own account (and reports vary on the exact arrival date in November 1903).
3. **The letter from abroad with a coupon** (Ch1 cliffhanger / Ch2 opening) is the commonly told origin story; the script says "a very lucky letter" without naming a country.
4. **Who was in the line** (clerks, factory workers, housemaids) - illustrative of the working people described in accounts, not a documented list.
5. **~$10 million by end of July, ~$20 million lost, under 30 cents on the dollar** - estimates vary between sources ($10-15M taken in; $15-20M lost).
6. **Arrest date** - sources say August 12 or 13, 1920; the script says "within days".
7. **Madoff ~$65 billion** = the fictitious balances on statements; the script says "statements showed ... most of it never existed" (actual losses were lower).
8. **Dramatised lines:** GUARD/PONZI ("Remember it. One day you'll read about me."), INVESTOR/PONZI ("my secret"), McMASTERS/EDITOR - consistent with the record (McMasters' story said Ponzi was at least $2M in debt), but the exact words are dramatised. The editor is not named on screen.
9. Per-American conversion of the FBI figure left out on purpose (reported losses only); add "about $19 per American" (6.57B ÷ ~340M) only if wanted.
